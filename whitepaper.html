    1. Project Overview

    What is Aritoham Protocol?

    Aritoham Protocol is presented by its team as a BNB Chain-based Web3 ecosystem built around token minting, token burning, staking participation, smart-contract-driven mechanisms, community participation, a reward architecture and future ecosystem development.

    MINT

    In the published model, tokens are created when users mint, within a $10–$1,000 range per mint.

    BURN

    The project states that tokens sold are burned, permanently removing them from circulation.

    GROW

    Participation in staking, the community and future ecosystem projects as the project develops.

    Vision & Mission

    Vision: a blockchain-based ecosystem with transparent, rule-based mechanisms, active community participation, long-term development and later expansion into Web3 and AI-related initiatives.

    Mission: to develop a connected Web3 ecosystem where users can understand and take part in token mechanisms, community programs and future digital services. No real-world adoption or completed achievements are claimed in this paper.

    2. Protocol Architecture

    BNB Chain foundation

    BNB Chain is a public blockchain: a shared ledger kept by many computers. A transaction is a signed instruction (for example, sending tokens) recorded permanently on that ledger. A smart contract is a program stored on the chain that runs automatically when its rules are met. Because records are public, anyone can inspect them with a block explorer such as BscScan. That openness is why on-chain transparency matters: you can check what happened rather than trust a description.

    Smart-contract role (conceptual)

    The model describes smart contracts as handling minting, burning, staking, reward calculation, transaction processing and protocol rules.

    4. How It Works

    Select a step to read it.

    Staking

    Staking means placing assets into a contract to take part in a protocol, in return for possible rewards. The deposit amount relates to participation in the published model. Before staking, understand lock periods, withdrawal rules and contract conditions. Lock periods and withdrawal features: requires confirmation.

    Claiming

    A claim is a request to receive rewards you are eligible for. Factors: how rewards are calculated, eligibility, whether a claim is available yet, on-chain transaction confirmation, and network fees (gas) paid in BNB. Operational claim conditions: requires confirmation.

    5. Mint & Burn Economy

    Minting

    Minting creates new tokens. It changes supply, so it matters who is permitted to mint. Check mint permissions in the contract.

    Burning

    Burning sends tokens to an address where they can never be used again, shrinking circulating supply. It differs from selling, where tokens move to a buyer. The project says 100% of tokens sold are burned. PUBLISHED CLAIM, VERIFY IN CONTRACT

    Burning alone does not guarantee a price rise. Price depends on demand, liquidity and market conditions.

    7. Security

    The project’s stated control philosophy is:

    No Admin

    Technically: no privileged account can change rules. Check for role-based permissions in the code.

    No Owner

    Technically: ownership renounced or never assigned. Check owner() on BscScan.

    No Limits

    Technically: no hard-coded caps on transfers. Compare with published limits such as the $10–$1,000 mint range.

    Controlled by Smart Contract

    Rules execute by code. Only verifiable by reading the deployed contract.
