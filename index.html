QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

A Web3 finance ecosystem where token creation, supply reduction, price support and utility growth are governed entirely by smart-contract logic — zero initial supply, smart minting, controlled burning and AI-powered intelligence.

Tokens are not pre-created. Supply begins at zero and expands only through on-chain participation — every mint is transparent, fully backed and verifiable in real time.

Every sell burns 100% of the tokens sold — permanently and irreversibly. Over time, controlled burning shrinks supply, creates scarcity and reinforces price from the inside.

Ownership is renounced — no admin can mint, pause or change the contract after deployment. Combined with anti-whale deposit caps, immutable code and independent audits, the protocol runs exactly as written.

Six stacked income streams: 1.25% daily emission rewards, 5% direct income, 30% generation income across 20 levels, 35% rank incentives, one-time rank rewards up to $125K, and the 5% DAO partner program.
